InquiTree

Evaluating AI Agents in the Scientific Inquiry Loop

Overview

InquiTree is a benchmark for evaluating AI agents across the full scientific inquiry loop, modeling research as an interactive process rather than a single-shot task. It is the first benchmark to cover all four stages of the inquiry cycle simultaneously.

4-Stage Inquiry Loop

Subtopic Proposal
Generating novel research directions and hypotheses
Study Design
Planning experiments, selecting methods, designing protocols
Study Execution
Running experiments, collecting data, executing code
Result Analysis
Analyzing data, drawing conclusions, evaluating findings

Key Findings

  1. Erosion of Marginal Capabilities: During long-horizon interactions, agents develop "cognitive tunneling," where critical judgment and anomaly detection degrade relative to their intrinsic baselines.
  2. Training Cutoff Boundary: Performance drops significantly on papers published after model training cutoff, revealing a boundary between interpolation and extrapolation.
  3. Inquiry-Loop Gap: Most prior benchmarks cover only 1-2 stages of the research process, leaving a critical gap in end-to-end evaluation.
  4. Scaling Limitation: Scaling context alone is insufficient for reliable AI scientists; stronger architectures or human oversight may be required to preserve critical evaluation and generalization.

Research Value

InquiTree addresses the critical gap in evaluating AI scientists across the complete research lifecycle, providing:

  • Stage-specific performance metrics for each inquiry loop phase
  • Cognitive diversity preservation measurement (avoiding tunneling)
  • Temporal generalization assessment (performance on new publications)
  • Framework for evaluating inquiry-style vs. single-shot research agents
